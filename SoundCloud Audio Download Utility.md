## 🌟 About

**SoundCloud Music Downloader — No Hidden Cost** is a clean, straightforward desktop app that downloads SoundCloud tracks and playlists to your computer in **original quality**. No sign-up, no watermark, no daily limit, no "premium unlock", no sketchy redirects.

Just paste a link, pick a folder, and download. It's the SoundCloud downloader you'd expect — with **zero surprises**.

> ⚠️ **Legal disclaimer:** This tool is intended for **downloading music you own or tracks explicitly offered for free download by the artist**, such as Creative Commons tracks, promotional releases, and self-published content. Downloading copyrighted tracks without permission may violate SoundCloud's Terms of Service and copyright law. **Always support the artists.**

## 🧩 Project Structure

```

├── 🎯 src/
```
